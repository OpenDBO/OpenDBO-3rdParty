D3D9 Platform Specific Overview

The following note applies to all versions of Direct3D and not just D3D9.

Note:
RenderWare Graphics in D3D9 will function under any video card. However D3D9 performance and support relies heavily on the video card drivers that are installed in the host machine. It is advised that you obtain the most recent drivers from your video card vendor for best results.
