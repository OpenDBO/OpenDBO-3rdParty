Destination Blending Modes
[D3D9]

The supported destination blending modes in D3D9 RenderWare Graphics are:

  • rwBLENDZERO
  • rwBLENDONE
  • rwBLENDSRCCOLOR
  • rwBLENDINVSRCCOLOR
  • rwBLENDSRCALPHA
  • rwBLENDINVSRCALPHA
  • rwBLENDDESTALPHA
  • rwBLENDINVDESTALPHA
  • rwBLENDDESTCOLOR
  • rwBLENDINVDESTCOLOR
  • rwBLENDSRCALPHASAT
