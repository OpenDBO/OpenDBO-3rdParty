ToonFX
[FX Pack]

ToonFX provides rendering of outlines and "cel shaded" texture based lighting to achieve cartoon-like 3D graphics.

  • RpToonArtistGuide.pdf includes Toon Editor and exporters
  • RpToonUserGuide.pdf includes RpToon
  • RpToon Plugin Overview
